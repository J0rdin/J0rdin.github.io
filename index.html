I'm Jordin Johns, a Game Artist focused on creating immersive visual experiences through 3D assets, environments and game-ready design.

I'm currently completing a Bachelor of Design in Interactive Game Art Design and Development at Jain Deemed-to-be University, Kochi. I enjoy moving between the artistic and technical sides of production — from shaping a model to building the world around it.

My work spans stylized props, vehicle modelling, digital sculpting, modular environments, character illustration and gameplay-focused UI.
